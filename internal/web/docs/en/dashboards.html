Dashboards and reports

Dashboards put the values you care about on one screen; reports show how available your devices were over a period.

Dashboards

Under Dashboards add a dashboard and click Edit. Widgets:

WidgetShows
Graphthe graph of a check, with the dashboard's time range
Current valueone value in big numbers, coloured by the state of its check
Device statesthe devices of a group (or all) as coloured tiles
Open problemsthe open problems, of a group or all
Mapa tile with the map's worst state that opens it

Widgets are small, medium or large in a grid; move them up and down. The dashboard reloads every minute, so it can stay open on a wall screen.

Availability reports

Reports shows for each device, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, this month or last month:

  • Availability — the share of the time the device was not down. Time when it was unreachable behind a down device counts as down; time in maintenance is shown apart and does not count.
  • Downtime, the number of problems and the average ping time.

Download it as CSV for a spreadsheet or print it or save it as PDF.
