Problems, alerts and notifications

Triggers watch the results of checks and open problems. A problem closes by itself when the condition ends; while it is open it can be acknowledged, and it is sent to your notification channels.

Triggers

Under Problems → Triggers. WatchZit starts with these:

Add your own: a value (CPU load, response time, traffic, connections …), above or below a threshold, for at least some minutes (a short dip does not open a problem), a severity, and whether it applies to all devices, a group or one device.

Problems

Problems lists what is open, worst first; the number in the menu counts the problems that were sent (red when one is critical). Acknowledge a problem with a note so others know someone is on it. Resolved problems stay in the history for a year.

Dependencies

Set Depends on on a device (for example the router of a branch office). When that router is down, the devices behind it are only unreachable: their problems are recorded but not sent — you get one message for the router, not fifty.

Maintenance

Under Problems → Maintenance plan a window for devices or groups. During it the devices are still monitored and problems recorded, but nothing is sent; problems still open when it ends are sent then. Availability reports show maintenance time apart.

Notifications

Under Settings → Notifications (administrators):

  • Email server — SMTP with STARTTLS, TLS or none; the password is stored encrypted. Send a test email.
  • Channels — email recipients, a webhook (JSON, signed with HMAC-SHA256 in X-WatchZit-Signature when you set a key), Slack or Microsoft Teams. Each channel has a lowest severity and can also report when problems are resolved.
  • Language of notifications and the console address used for links in them.
