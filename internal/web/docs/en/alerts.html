Problems, alerts and notifications

Triggers watch the results of checks and open problems. A problem closes by itself when the condition ends; while it is open it can be acknowledged, and it is sent to your notification channels.

Add your own: a value (CPU load, response time, traffic, connections …), above or below a threshold, for at least some minutes (a short dip does not open a problem), a severity, and whether it applies to all devices, a group or one device.

Problems

Problems lists what is open, worst first; the number in the menu counts the problems that were sent (red when one is critical). Acknowledge a problem with a note so others know someone is on it. Resolved problems stay in the history for a year.

Dependencies

Set Depends on on a device (for example the router of a branch office). When that router is down, the devices behind it are only unreachable: their problems are recorded but not sent — you get one message for the router, not fifty.

Maintenance

Under Problems → Maintenance plan a window for devices or groups. During it the devices are still monitored and problems recorded, but nothing is sent; problems still open when it ends are sent then. Availability reports show maintenance time apart.

Notifications

Under Settings → Notifications (administrators):

  • Email server — SMTP with STARTTLS, TLS or none; the password is stored encrypted. Send a test email.
  • Channels — email recipients, a webhook (JSON, signed with HMAC-SHA256 in X-WatchZit-Signature when you set a key), Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram (a bot token from @BotFather and the chat ID), ntfy (push to phones through ntfy.sh or your own ntfy server), PagerDuty (an Events API v2 integration key; problems open and resolve incidents), SMS through Twilio (account SID, auth token, sender, phone numbers) or SMS through your own gateway (any HTTP address with {text} where the message goes). Each channel has a lowest severity and can also report when problems are resolved.
  • Language of notifications and the console address used for links in them.

Escalation and on-call

Under Escalation and hours of duty of a channel:

  • Tell after (minutes) — the channel hears only of problems still open after that time, e.g. the team lead after 15 minutes when the first line has not reacted. Repeat every tells it again while the problem stays open.
  • Acknowledged problems are not escalated or repeated, unless Also problems someone acknowledged is on.
  • Only on some days and hours makes the channel an on-call duty: e.g. SMS to the night shift Monday–Friday from 18 to 08 and all weekend. Problems that start outside its hours reach it when its duty starts, if they are still open. For a rotation add one channel per person or team with its own days and hours.
  • A channel that was told about a problem is also told when it is resolved; the others are not.

Syslog and SNMP traps

Devices can send their log messages and SNMP traps to WatchZit. Switch it on under Settings → Syslog and traps: syslog on UDP 514 and traps (SNMP v1 and v2c, informs are answered) on UDP 162 by default, optionally only traps with given communities, kept for 30 days. Open these ports in the server's firewall and set WatchZit as the destination on the devices, e.g. on MikroTik /system logging action set remote remote=<server> and a logging rule with action remote, on FortiGate config log syslogd setting, on Linux an rsyslog *.* @<server>:514 line.

  • Logs lists the messages, newest first, with a search, a device, the source (syslog or traps) and the least important level (0 emergency … 7 debug). A message from an address that is no device is shown with its address. Each device page has a Logs button.
  • A trigger of the kind Syslog message or trap opens a problem when a message contains a text (or matches a regular expression between slashes, e.g. /link (down|failure)/) at a level at least as important as chosen. The problem stays open while such messages keep coming and closes when none came for the time of the trigger. These problems do not count as downtime in reports.
